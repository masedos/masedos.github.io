### Projects

#### Recommendation System — The Battle of Neighborhoods

Analyzed the relationship between New York City neighborhoods to recommend the best locations for a Brazilian restaurant. Key findings:

- Greenwich Village (Manhattan), West Village (Manhattan), and Astoria (Queens) are among the best neighborhoods for Brazilian cuisine.
- Manhattan has strong potential for the Brazilian restaurant market.
- Bedford-Stuyvesant ranks last in average rating of Brazilian restaurants.
- Manhattan is the best borough to be in if you prefer Brazilian cuisine.

[Report](https://masedos.github.io/Coursera_Capstone) · [Notebook](https://github.com/masedos/Coursera_Capstone/blob/main/Week%205/The_Battle_of_Neighborhoods_Week_2_Part_2.ipynb)

#### Classification — Titanic: Machine Learning from Disaster

Binary classification problem predicting Titanic passenger survival based on the classic [Kaggle competition](https://www.kaggle.com/c/titanic). Explored feature engineering opportunities across passenger demographics and travel data.

[GitHub](https://github.com/masedos/Titanic-Machine-Learning-from-Disaster) · [Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1ZtZKo-O-Xs_srY_-BKjsehtXJz0xDAT6?usp=sharing)
